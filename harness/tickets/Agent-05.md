# Agent-05. Authorize exact ticket-assigned plan-row content edits

## Intent

An authoritative ticket may need to change a specific content field in an existing `harness/plan.md` row. The role contracts must permit only that explicitly assigned content while keeping plan-row lifecycle ownership with the Orchestrator and preserving every other row and cell.

## Source

Human authorization: “A. Authorize a separate, bounded harness-policy refinement that explicitly permits ticket-assigned ledger-row deliverables with protected lifecycle hunks and selective staging; resume Dev-67 only after the applicable contracts are reconciled and committed.”

## Scope and contract

- Change only `harness/roles/orchestrator.md`, `harness/roles/implementor.md`, and `harness/roles/evaluator.md`. Reconcile every provision in those files about plan access, ledger status, staging and committing, evaluator inspection, and closeout that could conflict with this policy. Do not edit `harness/plan.md` content as an implementation deliverable in this ticket; the Orchestrator continues to own Agent-05's `in-progress` and terminal status transitions.
- Preserve the existing default protections: a ticket mention, an inferred need, a related row, or general permission to edit plan content does not authorize an Implementor to change `harness/plan.md`. Only the authoritative ticket may grant the exception, and only when its scope explicitly identifies the existing plan table/group and row by ticket ID, names each editable non-lifecycle column/cell, and specifies the intended content change. The row must exist at implementation entry. No row creation or deletion, row-ID/group change, or edit to an unnamed cell is authorized. The `Status` cell is never an Implementor-editable content cell.
- The Orchestrator alone owns lifecycle status. It sets the ticket row to `in-progress` before dispatch, keeps it `in-progress` through the Implementor's commit and evaluation, and applies any terminal status only in the separate Orchestrator closeout. The Implementor preserves that `in-progress` status and must not edit, stage, or commit the status cell. The Evaluator may verify the valid `in-progress` status but may not edit the ledger.
- For a ticket that does authorize plan-row content, before dispatch the Orchestrator must capture and retain (a) the committed `harness/plan.md` bytes at the recorded committed baseline SHA and (b) the exact implementation-entry worktree bytes after the Orchestrator's `in-progress` edit and before Implementor dispatch. Keep both snapshots as ignored run evidence and give their paths and the SHA to the Implementor and Evaluator. The Implementor and Evaluator must compare the exact authorized row and columns and all protected rows and cells against these baselines. Attribute the Orchestrator-owned lifecycle hunk separately; confirm it remains `in-progress` and that no other row or cell changed.
- The Implementor may stage only the explicitly authorized content cells using selective/patch staging. It must inspect the complete cached diff and verify that the `harness/plan.md` portion of its commit contains only those cells; the lifecycle hunk remains unstaged for Orchestrator closeout. Do not stage the whole file or use path-only staging/commit for `harness/plan.md`. Also verify the complete unstaged plan diff against the implementation-entry snapshot: it may contain only the Orchestrator-owned lifecycle hunk. If the authorized content and lifecycle hunks cannot be safely separated, stop and request Orchestrator disposition; do not broaden authorization or staging.
- Reconcile the existing plan-formatting instructions with protected-cell preservation: formatting must not change unauthorized rows or cells, and formatter output must not be committed as a separate cleanup that changes protected content. Compare formatter output with the retained implementation-entry bytes; format the authorized content while preserving protected bytes, and document any formatter difference confined to unchanged protected baseline content. If the authorized edit cannot be selectively formatted/staged without changing protected content, stop for Orchestrator disposition.
- When a ticket authorizes plan-row content, the Orchestrator replaces the default no-plan-access dispatch wording with the exact authorized row/cells, both baseline references, the Orchestrator-owned lifecycle hunk, and the selective-staging and cached-diff requirements. The Evaluator may inspect the exact authorized content and protected regions only to verify the ticket-specific change and preservation against both baselines; this grants no editing permission or general license to review unrelated plan content.
- Scope-gate allowances: `harness/roles/evaluator.md`. The Orchestrator owns this exact-path temporary allowance through implementation and evaluation and removes it at closeout. Do not change any other scope-gate allowance or gate policy as part of this ticket.
- Do not edit Dev-67 or any Dev-67 run or human-acceptance record. No product specifications, application code, tests, generated output, other role contracts, wrappers, or other harness files are in scope.

Named contract-review scenarios:

- **M1 — Exact authorization boundary:** review all three role contracts against both cases. An authoritative ticket naming an existing row by group and ID and naming one exact content cell with its intended change authorizes only that cell; a ticket that merely mentions the plan, omits the row or cell, names a nonexistent row, or implies another row/cell does not authorize any plan edit. In both cases the `Status` cell and all unnamed cells remain protected.
- **M2 — Baselines and selective commit:** verify the Orchestrator captures and supplies the committed baseline and post-lifecycle implementation-entry byte snapshots. For the allowed M1 case, compare every authorized and protected row/cell against both baselines, separately attribute the lifecycle hunk, and confirm the documented staging procedure commits only the authorized content, leaves the lifecycle hunk unstaged, and reviews the complete cached and unstaged diffs. The procedure must stop rather than relax boundaries when hunks cannot be separated.
- **M3 — Lifecycle ownership:** verify the role contracts preserve the Orchestrator's pre-dispatch `in-progress` update through implementation and evaluation, forbid Implementor status edits/staging/commits, require the Evaluator to check the valid `in-progress` state without editing, and reserve terminal status for a separate Orchestrator closeout.
- **M4 — Evaluator boundary:** verify the Evaluator's default status/index-only access remains unchanged unless the authoritative ticket grants the exact row/cell exception; under that exception it may inspect the authorized content and protected regions only for ticket-specific validation and preservation, and never edit.
- **M5 — Cross-contract and formatter consistency:** review the complete three role contracts and search their plan, ledger, status, staging, commit, evaluator-access, and closeout instructions. Reconcile all conflicting blanket provisions, including plan formatting, without weakening default protections or leaving contradictory directions. Confirm the only declared allowance is the exact temporary `harness/roles/evaluator.md` allowance and no Dev-67 record is in the deliverable diff.
